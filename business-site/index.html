What We Offer

Mobilized Media produces ticketed creative and social experiences, including Paint the Town sip-and-paint events. Customers purchase admission to scheduled in-person events. Ticket descriptions identify what is included with each admission option before checkout.

Prices are displayed in U.S. dollars (USD) at the point of sale. Payment is required at checkout; we do not offer a pay-later attendance option.

Paint the Town

Paint the Town is an Oakland creative social event experience featuring guided painting and community-focused entertainment. Event-specific dates, times, venue information, admission options, inclusions, and prices are provided on the applicable ticket-purchase page.

Where an event offers optional beverage or food-related add-ons, eligibility, availability, and event-specific restrictions are disclosed with the applicable offering. Guests must comply with venue rules and applicable law.

Refund & Dispute Policy

Refund eligibility is determined by the terms shown for the specific event or purchase. Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method when possible. A refunded or voided ticket or prepaid QR credential becomes invalid and cannot be used for admission or redemption. Customers who believe a charge is incorrect should contact Mobilized Media promptly so we can investigate before initiating a payment dispute.

Cancellation Policy

If Mobilized Media cancels an event, affected customers will be notified using the contact information supplied with their purchase and will receive the remedy stated in the event terms, which may include a refund or transfer. Customer-requested cancellations are governed by the refund/cancellation terms displayed for the applicable event.

Fulfillment

Our primary products are admissions and prepaid event items for in-person events. After successful online payment, customers receive or are provided access to their applicable electronic ticket, confirmation, or QR credential. Physical shipping is not ordinarily required for event admission.

Promotions & Discounts

Any promotion, coupon, or discount is subject to the conditions displayed with that offer, including any expiration date, eligible product or event, quantity limit, or other restriction. Promotions have no cash value unless expressly stated.

Privacy

Mobilized Media collects information reasonably necessary to process purchases, administer events, provide customer service, prevent fraud, and maintain business records. Payment-card information entered through Stripe Checkout is processed by Stripe; Mobilized Media does not require customers to send card numbers by email or social media. We do not sell customer payment information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the service or comply with law.
